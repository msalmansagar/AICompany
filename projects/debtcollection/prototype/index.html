Debt Collection Platform

A UX prototype for DCP-001 — one operational workspace over two Dynamics 365 CE organisations. Housing Loan CRM and BFD CRM each stay the system of record; a CRM Context Router decides where every request goes. 22 screens across seven files, sharing one design system. Self-contained: no build step, no network calls.
Read the badges. Every record shows HL or BFD — the organisation that owns it. Use the role picker in the header to switch between Collection Officer, RM, Senior Manager, Legal and Restructuring Officer; the sitemap and the command bars change with it. The theme picker offers Light, Dark, Glass and Vibrant.

What the prototype deliberately demonstrates

Cross-org Customer 360

Open Fatima Al-Sulaiti in Customer 360. She holds a home finance facility in Housing Loan CRM and a working-capital facility in BFD CRM. No single CRM query produces that view — the router fans out on QID and merges.

Server-side control, not UI theatre

Status transitions are validated by a CRM plugin, audit rows are written by a plugin, and stop-contact is enforced in the router. Try sending a message to Khalid Al-Hajri in the Communication console — the send is refused, and the refusal is logged.

Live versus warehouse

Operational Dashboards are labelled Live · both CRMs; Portfolio MIS is labelled DWH · nightly. Roll rates and league tables span both organisations and cannot come from either CRM.

Drift is surfaced, not hidden

Administration → RBAC reports that qdb_WorkoutOfficer exists in Housing Loan CRM but not in BFD CRM. Two systems of record mean two role definitions, and the difference is a finding.
